Section 1

Executive summary

BlueWhale Stack is not a single product — it is a unified, hyperscaler-neutral cloud management platform delivered in five editions, surrounded by a multi-tenant Partner Portal and a family of adjacent products (WhaleForge, Pandava OS, BlueWhale Fincore). The current website does not yet convey that scale: it reads as a basic brochure site and undersells a platform that competes with the likes of Flexera, ServiceNow CMP, Datadog and Terraform — combined.

This Scope of Work defines a world-class corporate website that matches the ambition of the product: a fast, credible, conversion-focused experience that lets a CIO, a FinOps lead, a datacenter operator, a government buyer, or a reseller each find their story within seconds — backed by interactive architecture, a clear edition comparison, deep module detail, and a dedicated partner journey.

5
platform editions to present clearly and honestly
17
platform modules across Foundation, Operations, Builder, Datacenter & AI
8
industry verticals with tailored regulatory narratives

What this document delivers

  • A complete content inventory of the platform, the five editions, every module, the Partner Portal, and the adjacent products — the raw material the site must present.
  • A full information architecture and a page-by-page scope for every page of the site.
  • A design system and a defined "world-class experience layer" that lifts the site from basic to best-in-class.
  • A technical approach, quality standards, and a phased plan with an indicative timeline (no commercials).
Foundation already in place. Phase 1 is built: a Next.js 15 + TypeScript application with a design system, a typed content layer, and data-driven Editions, Modules, Industries and Solutions pages already run locally. This SOW scopes the journey from that foundation to a launched, world-class site.

What makes a "best-in-class" enterprise platform site

The benchmark set (Stripe, Linear, Vercel, Datadog, ServiceNow) shares a recognisable pattern that this scope adopts:

  • Clarity over cleverness — a one-line value proposition the reader cannot misread.
  • Show the product — real UI, real architecture, not stock abstractions.
  • Layered depth — skimmers get the gist; evaluators can drill into module and architecture detail.
  • Speed & polish — instant loads, crisp typography, restrained motion that guides the eye.
  • Trust everywhere — security, compliance and outcomes woven through, not buried in a footer.

Section 4

Product & ecosystem overview

This section is the content the website must present. It is the single source of truth for the platform story, the five editions, the module catalog, the Partner Portal, and the adjacent products. The website's page copy derives from here.

4.1  The platform

BlueWhale Stack is a unified Cloud Management Platform (CMP) that consolidates inventory, provisioning, security & compliance, ITSM, observability, FinOps, migration and AI-driven operations into a single, hyperscaler-neutral control plane — across public cloud, private cloud, hybrid and on-premise datacenters. It replaces a stack of point tools (Datadog + ServiceNow CMP + Flexera + Terraform) with one platform, with Whale AI (Claude-powered) across every module rather than bolted on.

Hyperscaler-neutral

AWS, Azure, GCP, Oracle, IBM, Alibaba, plus VMware, Hyper-V, OpenStack & bare-metal.

Any operating model

SaaS, BYOC (your cloud), and air-gapped Sovereign deployment.

AI-native

Whale AI tiers — Spark, Tide, Abyss, Predictive-Ops — across the platform.

Per-edition detail

Standard

Single-region SaaS (Singapore). Modules: Identity, Cloud Connectors, Inventory, Provisioning, Audit Logs, Security & Compliance, Whale AI Spark. Limits: 1 tenant, 5 cloud accounts, 1,000 resources, 10 users.

Enterprise

Everything in Standard + Migration, Observe, ITSM (advanced), Landing Zone, WhaleForge, FinOps, Whale AI Spark/Tide/Abyss. Regions: SIN, BOM, FRA, LAX. SaaS / BYOC / Sovereign.

Datacenter

Enterprise + DCIM (site→room→row→rack→unit), Bare-Metal Lifecycle, Capacity Planning, Backup/DR, Predictive-Ops AI. Go edge agents (Redfish/IPMI/SNMP). Design partners include DataVolt, AdaniConneX, Reliance Jio.

Government & Telco

Government = Enterprise on air-gapped Sovereign deployment, in-region AI, no call-home. Telco & MSP = multi-tenant Enterprise with white-label tenant portals and per-tenant billing.

4.4  Partner Portal

The BlueWhale Stack Partner Portal is a separate, multi-tenant B2B SaaS — the sales & fulfillment backbone of the ecosystem. The website must give it a first-class presence: a partner-program narrative, a portal feature showcase, and an apply/onboarding entry point.

Who uses it: resellers (wholesale → resell), channel partners and MSPs (bundled solutions), tier-managed partners (Registered → Silver → Gold → Platinum), and BlueWhale internal staff (account managers, finance, partner managers, admins).

CapabilityWhat it does
Partner onboarding & tieringRegister partners, capture legal/tax details, assign tier with margin ceilings & SLA, track health score & lifecycle (Active / At-risk / Churned).
Deal registrationSubmit deals (BWS license / cloud service / consulting), workflow Submitted→Approved→Won/Lost, SLA-based approval deadlines & escalation, deal protection.
Customer managementOnboard end customers, allocate to partner/internal, multi-region tax-ID registry (GSTIN / TRN / EIN) with verification.
Margin & discount configPer-partner/product/cloud margin %, tier-ceiling enforcement, discount-request approval workflow, live price waterfall (customer price → margin → cost → profit).
Two-sided invoicing + tax engineBWS→Partner (wholesale) and Partner→Customer (retail) invoices; automatic tax by region (India IGST/CGST+SGST with HSN/SAC, UAE VAT, US sales tax); full invoice lifecycle.
Payments & reconciliationRazorpay (India), Stripe (UAE/US), manual bank transfer; payment links, webhook reconciliation, auto-mark paid.
Tenant provisioningProvision Standard/Enterprise tenants on payment or voucher; lifecycle gates; onboarding kit (credentials + welcome) auto-emailed; suspend/resume/extend/revoke.
Internal operationsFinancial planning dashboard, analytics, commission tracking with audit, centralized approval queue, immutable audit logging.

Tech (for context): React + Vite + TypeScript, Fastify API, PostgreSQL 16 + Prisma, Clerk auth, Resend email, Razorpay/Stripe, Cloudflare R2. The marketing site links to the portal; it does not re-implement it.

Section 8

Design system & brand

Modern-enterprise, refined — the altitude of Stripe / Linear / Vercel. Mostly light surfaces with selective dark hero/CTA drama.

Color

Brand blue #002da1 (primary), cyan/teal accent, a refined slate neutral scale, semantic success/warn. Dark "ink" surfaces for heroes & CTAs.

Typography

Display: Plus Jakarta Sans / Geist. Body: Inter / IBM Plex Sans. Fluid type scale via clamp().

Components

Button, Card, Badge/Pill, Stat, Tabs, Accordion, comparison table, logo cloud, breadcrumb, section heading, CTA band, placeholder — a documented library.

Layout & motion

12-column grid, generous whitespace, consistent section rhythm. Restrained motion: fade/slide on scroll, micro-interactions, reduced-motion honored.

Tokens are defined as Tailwind v4 @theme variables; the component library is reusable across every page for visual consistency.

Section 11

Technical architecture

Framework

Next.js 15 (App Router) + TypeScript + React 19. Server-rendered for SEO & speed; statically generated where possible.

Styling

Tailwind CSS v4 with @theme design tokens + a reusable component library (consistent with the product UI).

Content layer

Typed content in content/*.ts behind a single lib/content.ts access seam — a CMS (Directus or other) can replace the source later with zero component changes.

Forms

Next.js route handlers (app/api/*) for demo / contact / newsletter / careers; validation + email (Resend/SMTP); DB on deploy.

SEO & metadata

Per-page metadata, OpenGraph, JSON-LD (Organization/Product), sitemap.xml, robots.txt.

Hosting

Vercel, Railway, or Azure — decided at deploy-prep. Containerizable (Docker) if required.

CMS-ready by design. Content is self-contained today so the site runs with no external services, but the seam means editors can move to a headless CMS without a rebuild — your earlier "decide later" choice, preserved.
